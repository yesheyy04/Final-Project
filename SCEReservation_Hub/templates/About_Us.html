About Us

Welcome to the Samtse College of Education Sports Court & Rooms Reservation System! Our platform makes it easy and accessible for students, staff, faculty, and guests to reserve sports facilities and rooms. From basketball courts and football grounds to multimedia rooms and guest house, bookings are fast, fair, and transparent.

Our Mission

To encourage active participation in sports, fitness, and collaborative activities by providing a simple and reliable reservation system.

What We Offer

  • Sports Courts – Basketball, football, volleyball, and badminton hall reservations.
  • Rooms & Halls – Multimedia room, guest houses reservations.
  • Fair Access – Equal opportunities for all users.
  • Convenience – Quick bookings with real-time availability.
